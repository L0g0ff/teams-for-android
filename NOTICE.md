# Artwork notice

The Teams for Android launcher icon is an original derivative inspired by the composition of the [Teams for Linux](https://github.com/IsmaelMartinez/teams-for-linux) launcher icon: a purple `T` tile paired with a platform mascot.

Teams for Linux states that its icons come from [Icon Duck](https://iconduck.com/sets/hugeicons-essential-free-icons) under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Changes made for this project:

- Tux was removed.
- An original Android-inspired robot was drawn for this project.
- Shapes, proportions and launcher-size assets were recreated for Android.
